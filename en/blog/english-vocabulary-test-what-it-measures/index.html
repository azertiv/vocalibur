Learning English

English vocabulary test: what our quiz really measures

3 min readReviewed October 26, 2026Method and corrections

Translated and adapted from French by the Vocalibur team. Sources are cited in their original language.

Key points

  • The quiz shows your correct answers out of eighteen, with no extrapolation.
  • Chance, distractors and the choice of words limit how the result can be read.
  • Your answers stay in the browser and are not passed on to the app.

A useful result starts with a precise question

When a website tells you that you know several thousand words, what is it counting? Written forms, dictionary entries, word families or meanings? Does it ask you to recognize a definition or to produce an answer? Without this information, a large number can seem informative while remaining hard to interpret.

A vocabulary test measures performance on a set of tasks. To extrapolate to a whole population of words or compare people, it also needs a suitable sampling method, scoring system and validation data. A polished presentation doesn’t replace this work.

What the Vocalibur quiz offers

The quiz on this site contains eighteen English words. For each one, you choose a definition from four options or say that you don’t know. The questions and definitions were written for this quiz. The words appear in an order of increasing difficulty set by our editors, shuffled within small groups; this order is not a validated psychometric scale.

The result shows only the number of correct answers out of eighteen, followed by the expected definitions and your choices when they were wrong. It gives no CEFR level, no total vocabulary size and no percentile. It doesn’t reuse the scoring of a published scientific instrument. The answers stay in your browser’s memory and the quiz neither sends nor saves them.

Why we don’t extrapolate

With four options, chance can produce a correct answer. The distractors, meaning the wrong answers offered, also affect the difficulty. A word that is familiar in one sense can be unknown in another. Finally, eighteen entries chosen by editors are not a representative sample of all English vocabulary.

So a score of 12 out of 18 means that twelve answers were correct in this series, not that you know two thirds of English. Taking the same quiz again may mostly measure how well you remember the questions or the corrections.

Recognizing is not producing, and vocabulary is not the whole language

The translation tests in Webb (2008) show that the gaps between receptive and productive knowledge depend in particular on how strict the scoring is. Our quiz doesn’t ask you to retrieve the spelling of a word without help. Nor does it assess listening comprehension, speaking or writing, grammar or the ability to hold a conversation.

The CEFR describes broader language skills. A series of vocabulary questions isn’t enough to assign an overall level. The quiz on this site is also separate from the app’s estimated vocabulary, which starts from the words you say you know when you set up the app: no quiz result is passed on to Vocalibur.

How to get something out of it despite these limits

Use the correction as a starting point. Choose two words you would really like to use. Close the result, retrieve their meaning, then write an original sentence for each. Check the pattern and the meaning with a dictionary or with someone who knows the language well. Come back to these words a few days later in a new situation.

A correct answer reached by elimination can deserve as much attention as a mistake. On the other hand, a rare word unrelated to your needs doesn’t have to become a priority. The most useful thing the quiz can give you is a few words or uses to explore, rather than a ranking.

You can open the quiz or read what counting words means to better understand the different units of measurement.

Frequently asked questions

Why don’t I get a total number of words I know?
The eighteen words were not calibrated to estimate all your vocabulary knowledge. An extrapolation would give a misleading sense of precision.
Is my result sent or saved?
No. The current quiz runs in your browser’s memory. It neither sends nor keeps your answers and doesn’t create a profile in the app.
