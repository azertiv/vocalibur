The 1,000 most common English words

Ranked from the most frequent to the rarest, with part of speech, level and a short definition. Together they make up 82% of the words in an ordinary English text.

This list is the first 1,000 entries of Vocalibur’s path, in the order of the app’s frequency tables, calculated on books and film dialogue. As in the app, a word comes back once for each clearly distinct meaning: “right” appears four times (correct, the right-hand side, an entitlement, the political right), and so does “well” (in a good way, a remark opener, healthy, a hole dug for water). Each row gives that meaning.

Reading a list is not enough to remember a word. In Vocalibur, each word comes in a sentence and returns just when you are about to forget it.
